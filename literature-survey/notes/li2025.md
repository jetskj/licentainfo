# Li, Dutta & Naik (2025) — IRIS: LLM-Assisted Static Analysis for Detecting Security Vulnerabilities

**Key:** `li2025` · **Type:** conference paper, ICLR 2025 · **DOI:** [10.48550/arXiv.2405.17238](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2405.17238) · **PDF:** `papers/li2025_iris.pdf` (ICLR proceedings; arXiv:2405.17238)

**Cluster:** Detection · **Used in:** Related work (upstream half); Discussion

## What it establishes

Program-analysis tools for vulnerability detection are limited by their reliance on **human-labelled specifications**; LLMs alone cannot do whole-repository reasoning. IRIS is a **neuro-symbolic** approach that combines LLMs with static analysis:

- LLMs infer **taint specifications** and perform contextual analysis;
- static analysis does the whole-repository reasoning.

The authors curate **CWE-Bench-Java**: **120 manually validated vulnerabilities** in real-world Java projects.

## Numbers worth citing

- CodeQL detects **27 of 120** vulnerabilities.
- IRIS with GPT-4 detects **55** (+28).
- IRIS improves CodeQL's average **false discovery rate by 5 percentage points**.
- IRIS identifies **4 previously unknown vulnerabilities**.

## Why it matters for this thesis

Three uses:

1. **The upstream half is not solved either.** Even a strong static analyser finds under a quarter of the curated vulnerabilities, and the LLM-assisted system finds under half. A repair pipeline that depends on detection inherits that recall ceiling.
2. **False discovery rate is a first-class metric.** IRIS reports it explicitly; the thesis reports false accepts and false rejections separately for the same reason — the error direction matters.
3. **Combination beats either component.** IRIS is evidence that heterogeneous checks add value, which is the thesis's premise for composing O0–O4 rather than picking one.

It also supports the thesis's decision to drop CodeQL as a *judge*: CodeQL's weakness in the relevant category (access control) is structural, and IRIS's gains come from adding LLM reasoning, not from the analyser alone.

## Where to use it

- Related work, detection subsection.
- Discussion: why the thesis does not use a SAST tool as the gate.
- Introduction: one sentence that detection and acceptance are both unreliable, but the thesis measures the latter.

## Limits

Java, detection not repair, and the benchmark is Java-specific. The numbers are for a particular CodeQL version and GPT-4 configuration; do not generalise them to other stacks or models.
